How this comparison works · scope and sources

Designed only for electrical tradespeople who qualify for the Cbus Electech occupation category. Cbus cover starts at its automatic two Death units and two TPD units. Moving either slider or entering a dollar amount changes that cover independently in whole units.

Dollar entries are rounded down to the closest whole Cbus unit. Death cover is capped at $5 million and TPD cover at $3 million. The estimated Cbus premium is the applicable weekly unit rate multiplied by the selected number of units.

Brighter cover is five times its published Death and TPD unit value for the member's age next birthday. Its fixed comparison cost is the Blue Collar rate of $1.459 per unit per week, or $7.295 for five units. Cover and premiums are estimates only and do not compare definitions, eligibility, underwriting, exclusions, tax treatment or claim outcomes.

Rates used: Brighter Super Insurance Guide – Energy Industry, issued 1 October 2025; Cbus Insurance Guide (Industry Super), dated 1 July 2026. Check current fund documents before relying on the result.
