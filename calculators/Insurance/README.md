# Electrical Trades Insurance Cover Comparison

Upload this complete folder to `calculators/Comparison/Insurance Cover Comparison` in the existing GitHub Pages repository.

The calculator:

- uses the existing Supabase authentication configuration;
- returns to the dashboard at `../../../index.html`;
- includes its own copy of `shared-theme.css`;
- compares Brighter Super five-unit Death and TPD cover with adjustable Cbus Electech cover; and
- uses whole Cbus units, the $5 million Death limit and the $3 million TPD limit.

After uploading the folder, add or retain this dashboard link:

```html
<a href="calculators/Comparison/Insurance Cover Comparison/index.html" class="card">🛡️ Electrical Trades Insurance Cover Comparison</a>
```

Rates are a dated snapshot from the Brighter Super Energy Industry Insurance Guide issued 1 October 2025 and the Cbus Industry Super Insurance Guide dated 1 July 2026. Review the calculator when either fund updates its rates or cover values.
