HOW THIS CALCULATOR WORKS · RULES AND LIMITATIONS
  • Reaching age 65 is modelled as a condition of release with no cashing restriction, whether or not employment continues.
  • New contributions are credited to accumulation. A scheduled addition commences a new retirement-phase income stream and is displayed within one combined income-stream balance.
  • The fixed 2026–27 scenario uses a $32,500 concessional cap, $130,000 annual non-concessional cap and $2.1 million general transfer-balance cap. These amounts are not indexed in later projection years.
  • Bring-forward availability is based on opening total super balance: up to $390,000 below $1.84 million, $260,000 from $1.84 million to below $1.97 million, $130,000 from $1.97 million to below $2.1 million, and nil at or above $2.1 million.
  • Current income-stream balance does not determine transfer-balance cap already used. Pension investment growth and ordinary pension payments do not change historical cap usage.
  • Voluntary contributions stop from age 75 in the annual model. Compulsory employer contributions continue. The precise 28-day birthday-month window is not modelled.
  • When total desired annual income is selected, take-home pay is counted first and only the remaining amount is requested from the income stream, subject to the statutory pension minimum. Minimum pension payments use the opening income-stream balance after any scheduled start-of-year addition. Commencement-year pro-rating and fund processing delays are not modelled.
  • The salary-funded target first uses concessional cap space remaining after employer contributions. Any target above that space is treated as an after-tax contribution, subject to available take-home pay and the non-concessional/bring-forward cap. Personal deductible amounts that fail the work test are tested as non-concessional instead.
  • Employer contributions above the concessional cap remain excess concessional contributions. The estimate retains them in super after marginal tax is released, and counts them against non-concessional cap space. Additional excess non-concessional tax is outside the model.
  • Estimated take-home pay uses 2026–27 resident tax rates plus a full 2% Medicare levy. It excludes offsets, deductions, HELP repayments, Medicare reductions/exemptions and other income.
  • Division 293 is a salary-only estimate. Division 296 tax is excluded.
  • Investment choices load from investment-returns.js, so published figures can be refreshed without editing the calculator engine. Historical performance is not a forecast.

General scenario tool only. It does not account for every personal transfer-balance event, tax component, carry-forward concessional amount, fund rule, or future legislative change.
